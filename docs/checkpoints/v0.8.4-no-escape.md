# Math Arcade v0.8.4 — closing a way to play without math (implementation checkpoint)

Owner, 2026-10-15: students were going back, reloading and reopening the window to avoid questions.

## Server audit (`server/core-model.mjs`), checked with a scripted probe

| Escape attempt | Result before | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Reload / back / close and reopen during a math gate | Same question returned (gate and question are stored on the server, not the iPad) | Unchanged |
| Leave during a gate | Gate and question kept | Unchanged |
| Pick a different game during a gate | Refused: "Finish or leave your current activity first." | Unchanged |
| **Start Kingsgate (no opening gate), leave before the question round, start Kingsgate again** | **Worked — unlimited play without math** | **Closed:** leaving Kingsgate early means the next Kingsgate start needs a normal math gate; the start screen tells students so |

Not covered by this change: Chrono Circuit and Olivia keep their own native gates, and Math Civ has built-in math by design. A student can still join under a classmate's name; the teacher sees the name, and a second device for the same student is flagged.
